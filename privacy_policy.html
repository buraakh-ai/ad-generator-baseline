Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Ad Generator application ("the App") collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and how you can request that it be deleted.

Information We Collect

To generate and publish ads on your behalf, the App collects and processes:

  • Company information — the company name, website URL, and product/ad details you provide when generating an ad.
  • Generated content — ad copy and ad images created by the App based on your input.
  • Social media access tokens — OAuth access tokens for the social media accounts you connect (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn), used solely to authorize posting to those accounts.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or private messages from any connected account.

How We Use This Information

Information collected by the App is used exclusively to:

  • Generate ad copy and ad images tailored to the company information you provide.
  • Publish that generated content to the social media accounts you have explicitly connected and authorized, at your direction.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How We Store This Information

Generated ad images and related assets are stored in a private Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3 bucket. Access tokens are stored only as required to perform the posting actions you request, and are not shared with any party other than the social media platform they belong to (e.g. an Instagram token is only ever sent to Instagram's API).

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain generated content and access tokens only for as long as needed to provide the App's functionality to you. You may request deletion of your data — including generated ad assets and any stored access tokens — at any time.

To request deletion, email buraqconsulting.ai@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and the company name or account details associated with your usage of the App. Requests will be processed within 30 days.

Revoking Access

You can revoke the App's access to your social media accounts at any time directly from that platform's settings (e.g. Instagram/Facebook: Settings → Apps and Websites; LinkedIn: Settings → Data Privacy → Permitted Services). Revoking access immediately prevents the App from posting on your behalf.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at buraqconsulting.ai@gmail.com.
